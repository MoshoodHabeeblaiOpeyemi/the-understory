You open two tabs every day. In one, you read articles and look at
pictures. In the other, you send messages, edit documents, and drag
things around. Both live in the browser — but they are different
animals.

## A website is a magazine. A web app is a machine

A **website** is built to be *read*. It serves pages of words and
images, and your clicks mostly move you from one page to another —
like flipping through a magazine.

A **web app** is built to be *used*. Gmail, Google Docs, online
games. Your clicks don't just change the page — they change *data*:
a message is sent, a document is saved, a score is recorded.

The two are not rivals, they are ingredients:

- A website gives you: reading, pages, calm, focus
- A web app gives you: buttons, saving, memory, feedback

**The Understory** is mostly a website — you will read 167 lessons.
But it quietly borrows app tricks: a "mark as complete" button,
progress bars that remember you. Modern sites are recipes, mixing
both ingredients in different amounts.
